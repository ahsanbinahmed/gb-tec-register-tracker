## Methodology and caveats

- Read original archived files `tec_2026-11-12.csv` and `tec_2026-11-19.csv`, using the repository's established project-number normalisation (remove stage suffix from `PRO-...`), highest-gate project classification, and exclusion of blank project keys.
- The intervening archive shows changes during the week; this report deliberately compares endpoints and does not infer the exact timing of each project's movements from those endpoints alone.
- Project-total MW is the highest `Cumulative Total Capacity (MW)` across the project's stages. **Do not sum project-total MW with stage-level Gate 2 MW.**
- For continuity with the previous public report, Gate 2 row-increment MW sums `MW Increase / Decrease` for Gate 2-tagged rows. The local analyser's separate fallback series substitutes cumulative capacity where a collapsed-stage gate has zero increments.
- Register disappearance can reflect administrative or identification changes; it does not prove withdrawal or cancellation. Gate status does not demonstrate investment sanction or energisation.
- This is an independent longitudinal analysis of published NESO data, with AI-assisted calculation and drafting.
