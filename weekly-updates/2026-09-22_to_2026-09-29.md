# TEC weekly update: 22–29 September 2026

## Headline

Gate 2 increased from **98 to 99 projects**, with row-level Gate 2 capacity rising from **14,435 MW to 14,498 MW**.

## Gate movements

Benthead Solar moved from Unassigned to Gate 2, adding **63 MW** of observed Gate 2 capacity.

Seven projects moved from Unassigned to Gate 1, representing **709 MW project capacity**, including Lambeeth 240MW (240 MW), Gastard BESS (200 MW), AGS Coatbridge (60 MW), Kirkton Wind Farm (60 MW), AGS Nerston BESS (50 MW), Crookedstone PV (50 MW) and Dougliehill BESS (49 MW).

Equinor Celtic Sea 2 moved from Gate 1 to Unassigned. Melksham Grid Park ESS & PV was no longer present in the later snapshot. Two new battery projects appeared as Unassigned.

## Analytical note

This was a modest Gate 2 week compared with 15–22 September, but Gate 1 continued to change materially beneath the headline Gate 2 total.
