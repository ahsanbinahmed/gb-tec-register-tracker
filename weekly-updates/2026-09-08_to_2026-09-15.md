# TEC weekly update: 8–15 September 2026

## Headline

The number of projects classified at Gate 2 remained **90**, while row-level Gate 2 capacity fell from **13,468 MW to 13,335 MW**.

## Gate movements

Four projects moved from Unassigned to Gate 1, totalling **207 MW project capacity**:

- South Street BESS: 63 MW
- SEC 1 BESS: 60 MW
- Cleveden Road BESS: 43 MW
- Coldstream Solar PV Plant: 41 MW

Two 1,800 MW Xlinks entries, Alverdiscott and Alverdiscott 2, were no longer present in the later snapshot.

The Gate 2 row-level capacity associated with North Kyle New Cumnock reduced by **133 MW** between snapshots.

## Analytical note

This was a comparatively quiet Gate 2 week by project count, but it demonstrates why total Gate 2 MW can change even when the number of Gate 2-classified projects does not.
