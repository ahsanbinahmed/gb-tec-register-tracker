# AI Assistance Disclosure

This project is intentionally transparent about the role of AI.

The automation, PowerShell/Python scripts and parts of the analytical workflow were developed with substantial assistance from generative AI tools.

The project owner:

- identified the TEC Register as a dataset worth tracking over time
- initiated the archive in August 2026
- selected the analytical questions and outputs of interest
- maintained and reviewed the recurring process
- examined anomalies and methodological issues
- uses the resulting evidence in ongoing GB energy-market analysis and communication

Accordingly, this repository should be read as evidence of **AI-assisted analytical problem solving and energy-market investigation**, not as a claim that every line of code was independently authored from first principles.

All published findings remain subject to checking against the underlying NESO data and the methodology documented in this repository.
