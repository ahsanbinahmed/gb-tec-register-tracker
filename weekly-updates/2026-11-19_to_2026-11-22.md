## Methodology and limitations

Projects are normalised using the base PRO-number, collapsing multi-stage rows. Project-total capacity is the maximum cumulative capacity recorded across project stages, not the sum of stages. Gate classification uses the highest recorded gate. Gate movements are endpoint comparisons; intermediate movements are not independently verified. Project-total MW associated with movements is **not** newly granted connection capacity. A disappeared listing does not establish cancellation, and gate status does not confirm construction.

This report publishes aggregated analysis only; the underlying historical project-level archive remains private.
