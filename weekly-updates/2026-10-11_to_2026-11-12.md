This is an independent assessment of changes in the published register, not evidence of physical completion, investment sanction, or energisation.

## Movements between the endpoint snapshots

- **36 continuing projects** changed from unassigned to Gate 2, representing **9,075 MW in project-total capacity**. The project-total measure is *not* equivalent to MW of Gate 2-tagged stages.
- **83 continuing projects** changed from unassigned to Gate 1, representing **22,934 MW project-total capacity**.
- **One project** changed from Gate 1 to unassigned (740 MW project-total).
- **Two unassigned projects** disappeared from the endpoint register (967 MW project-total).
- **Three projects** appeared in the endpoint register: two at Gate 1 (1,550 MW project-total) and one unassigned (40 MW project-total). Appearing in this comparison is not synonymous with being newly conceived or approved.
- **81 continuing projects** had a changed earliest recorded MW effective date: **55 later** and **26 earlier**.

Counts reconcile: opening 2,082 + 3 appearing - 2 disappearing = 2,083. Gate counts reconcile to 2,083 at the endpoint.

## Methodology and limitations

- Source: archived NESO TEC CSVs dated `2026-10-11` and `2026-11-12`; the archive has **94 successful dated snapshots from 9 August to 12 November 2026**, missing **19 August** and **26 September**.
- Identical project identifiers with stage suffixes are grouped under a single base `PRO-...` number. Blank project keys are excluded. A project's highest recorded gate determines its project-level classification.
- Capacities stated for individual projects and transition groups are **project-total figures**, measured using their highest cumulative capacity, *not* stage-specific gated MW.
- The headline Gate 2 MW figure uses the **increment-only row sum** employed in the previous public update, maintaining numerical continuity. This method can understate capacity when stages are collapsed and an increment becomes zero; the improved local analyser has a fallback for those cases, giving **16,849 MW** (11 October) and **26,460 MW** (12 November) as *separate, non-comparable-to-previous-report* estimates. Do not mix these definitions in trend charts.
- Endpoint comparisons cannot tell whether a project moved more than once in between. Nor do they establish whether an administrative update reflects a new application, construction progress or an investment decision.
- Raw archived CSVs, detailed historical outputs and internal analysis scripts remain outside this public repository.

*Independent analysis of published NESO TEC Register data. AI-assisted calculations and drafting, with explicit methodological limitations.*
